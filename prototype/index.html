Who We Are

From a Derg-era underground prayer group to a global ministry.

Our Vision

"To reach the unreached, build the body of Christ with biblical excellence, and establish a legacy of faith across Ethiopia and the world."

8 Core Ministry Values

Biblical Truth

The Word of God is our ultimate authority for life and ministry.

Christ-Centered Worship

Our primary goal is to glorify Jesus Christ in all things.

Fervent Prayer

Intercession is the engine of everything we do.

Spirit of Excellence

Giving our absolute best to God's service.

1970s

Underground Beginnings

Founded as a clandestine prayer group during the Derg regime, meeting in private homes to avoid persecution while growing in the Spirit.

1991

Public Expansion

Following political changes, Gospel Light emerged publicly, establishing its first official worship center at Aware, Addis Ababa.

2005 - 2026

Global Reach

Opening branches across Ethiopia's diverse synods and establishing a vibrant North American presence in Washington, D.C.

Apostle Daniel Makonnen

Founder & General Overseer

A visionary leader whose ministry has impacted millions across the globe. Apostle Daniel is known for his deep biblical insight and passion for raising the next generation of leadership in Ethiopia.

Pastor Aberham Bedasso

Senior Pastor, Washington D.C. Branch

Leading the North American expansion with a focus on diaspora ministry, spiritual maturity, and intergenerational worship. Pastor Aberham brings the Gospel Light to the heart of the U.S. capital.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, only infallible, authoritative Word of God.

The Trinity

We believe there is one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Salvation

We believe that for the salvation of lost and sinful people, regeneration by the Holy Spirit is absolutely essential.
